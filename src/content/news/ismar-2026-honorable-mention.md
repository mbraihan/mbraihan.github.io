I am thrilled to share that our paper, *"Evaluating the Vergence-Accommodation Conflict in Gaze-Based 3D Target Selection,"* has received a **Best Paper Award Honorable Mention** at IEEE ISMAR 2026!

You can find more details about the award and the list of recipients on the [IEEE ISMAR 2026 Awards page](https://www.ieeeismar.net/2026/awards/).

This work investigates the fundamental effects of visual depth on eye-gaze tracking interaction in Virtual Reality. I want to thank my co-authors for their incredible collaboration and the reviewers for their constructive feedback.
