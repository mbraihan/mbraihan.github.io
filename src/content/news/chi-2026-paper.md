Our paper, *"Eyes on Many: Evaluating Gaze, Hand, and Voice for Multi-Object Selection in Extended Reality,"* has been accepted at the ACM Conference on Human Factors in Computing Systems (CHI 2026).

The study compares three input modalities for selecting multiple objects in XR. The reported paper acceptance rate was **25.3%**.
