Our paper, *"Effects of Visual Depth on Vergence–Accommodation Conflict in Gaze- and Controller-Based Selection in Virtual Reality,"* has been accepted as a poster at CHI 2026.

The work examines how visual depth affects gaze- and controller-based selection under vergence–accommodation conflict. The reported poster acceptance rate was **38.4%**.
