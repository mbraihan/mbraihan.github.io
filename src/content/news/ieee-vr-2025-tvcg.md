Our paper on early-warning systems for hand-tracking failure has been accepted to the IEEE VR 2025 **TVCG journal track**.

The work investigates how immersive systems can detect and communicate impending hand-tracking breakdowns before they disrupt interaction. The reported acceptance rate was approximately **17%**.
