Our paper, *"Crossing Realities Has a Cost: Evaluating AR Second Screens for AI-Assisted Desktop Authoring,"* has been accepted at the ACM Symposium on Spatial User Interaction (SUI 2026).

The study examines the benefits and interaction costs of moving between a conventional desktop workspace and an augmented-reality second screen during AI-assisted authoring tasks.
