Our paper, *"Play and Learn: Gamified Feedback for Ultrasound-Guided Catheter Insertion Training in Virtual Reality,"* has been accepted at IEEE SEGAH 2026.

This work is an outcome of my Mitacs Accelerate internship and explores how gamified feedback can support learning in immersive medical training.
