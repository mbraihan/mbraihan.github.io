Our paper, *"Evaluating Vergence–Accommodation Conflict in Gaze-Based 3D Target Selection,"* has been accepted to the **IEEE Transactions on Visualization and Computer Graphics (TVCG) track** at IEEE ISMAR 2026.

The study examines how vergence–accommodation conflict affects gaze-based target selection in three-dimensional environments. The reported acceptance rate was approximately **16%**.
