Our paper, *"Depth3DSketch: Freehand Sketching Out of Arm’s Reach in Virtual Reality,"* has been accepted to the Late-Breaking Work track at CHI 2025.

The project investigates freehand sketching techniques that let people create and manipulate content beyond their immediate physical reach in VR.
