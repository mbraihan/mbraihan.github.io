Our paper, *"There Is More to Dwell Than Meets the Eye: Toward Better Gaze-Based Text Entry Systems With Multi-Threshold Dwell,"* has been accepted at CHI 2025.

The study explores how multiple dwell thresholds can improve the design of gaze-based text-entry systems. The reported paper acceptance rate was approximately **25%**.
