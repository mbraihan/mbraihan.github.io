## Introduction

Scaling objects in 3D environments often requires bimanual interactions. In this post, we explore unimanual scaling techniques leveraging gaze selection combined with pinch gestures.

## Key Techniques

- **uniDepth**: Scaling based on depth ray displacement.
- **uniAngle**: Angle-based rotation scaling.
- **uniGrow**: Proportional distance expansion from gaze centroid.
