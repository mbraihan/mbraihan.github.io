tldr: "Examines how EMG artefacts affect EEG-based video-category classification and finds that contaminated signals can carry additional discriminating information."
abstract: >-
  EEG signals are highly susceptible to noise, and mixtures of electrooculography (EOG) and electromyography (EMG) artefacts with EEG are inevitable. This paper presents findings on the effects of EMG artefacts on EEG signals used to categorize videos. The experiments suggest that, for video-category classification using EEG, signals with EMG artefacts have more discriminating capacity.
